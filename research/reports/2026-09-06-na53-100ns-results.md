## 5. Thermodynamics — ensemble control

| Quantity | mean | min | max | stdev | final | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature (K) | 310.14 | 308.30 | 312.27 | 0.59 | 310.59 | ~310.15 ✓ |
| Pressure (bar) | **1.99** | −158.52 | 166.83 | 50.72 | 61.03 | ~1.0 (mean) — see caveat |
| Density (kg/m³) | **2.62** | −102.13 | 146.38 | 36.12 | −26.60 | **~1000 — this looks wrong, see caveat** |
| Potential (kJ/mol) | −4,090,223 | −4,096,999.5 | −4,082,485.75 | 2,358.84 | −4,090,384.75 | large negative stable well ✓ |

**Temperature** is clean. **Pressure mean ≈ 2 bar** is close to the 1 bar target — the large instantaneous swings (±150+ bar) are normal for Parrinello-Rahman on a solvated system; the mean is what matters and it's near target.

**Two caveats on the energy terms:**

1. **Density is clearly wrong as reported** (mean ~2.6 kg/m³, range crossing zero). The analysis script uses gmx 2024.x term ID `36` for Density per its own comment, but the result is unphysical. Most likely causes:
   - GROMACS 2024.4's `gmx energy` term numbering for this build differs from the IDs the script assumes (the earlier pressure bug was exactly this class of issue), or
   - term 36 on this build is a virial/dispersion component, not mass density.
   - **Action:** verify on T3 with `gmx energy -f prod.edr` (interactive term list) and re-extract with the correct Density ID before trusting the density panel in `energy_terms.png`.
2. **Pressure mean ~2 bar is believable but should be re-confirmed from the corrected term** once the ID issue is resolved for density — same verification pass. The instantaneous pressure trace in the log is internally consistent with a near-1-bar ensemble.

Potential energy is a deep, stable negative well (~−4.09e6 kJ/mol), which is the expected sign and magnitude for a solvated biomolecular system. That's the correct "shape" even if the absolute number isn't a physical observable by itself.
